New social_dispatches is a durable outbox with unique per-connection/account/variant/revision/schedule identity, current campaign version, exact approval/profile and approved destination_key==connection_id. Claim/start recheck owner, connection version/expiry/scopes, revision/approval/schedule/profile and media publish rights before sending. Schedule alone is not consent or success.

runDue selects a bounded due batch; concurrent dispatchers cannot claim one queued item twice. resumeUnsent replaces only pre-send claimed fences; old workers fail before publish. Sending/uncertain outcomes never gain a new publishing attempt: restart invokes connector.reconcile with durable identity/reference, not publish. A confirmed platform ID+approved public URL+publication time is required for published; accepted HTTP/pending/timeout remains reconciliation_required. Callback replay is idempotent; conflicting final result fails. Cancellation fences only queued/claimed work; no false cancellation after sending. Append-only audit preserves transitions.

Owner writes, scoped member reads; no raw token/fence/source payload disclosed. No worker-run/claim/finish or raw callback-forgery HTTP route. All routes default503 with no injected service.
